# University of Nottingham website

Static six-page HTML, CSS and JavaScript website in `dist/`.

## Pages

- `dist/index.html` — home
- `dist/about.html` — about
- `dist/accreditations.html` — quality assurance
- `dist/programs.html` — study areas and application FAQ
- `dist/why-us.html` — why Nottingham
- `dist/student-reviews.html` — student voices

## Notes for content owners

This is a design concept, not an official University publication. The sample uses source-linked student stories and Students’ Union newsletter links. Ratings, accreditations, rankings, partner names, leadership details, course availability, and newsletter collection are marked as placeholders where current approved data was not available. Replace those fields with approved material before any public launch.

Facts used for the concept include the University College opening in 1881, its Royal Charter in 1948, the Malaysia campus beginning in 2000, first students at Ningbo in 2004, three countries with campuses, and an approximate 40,000+ student community. See:

- University of Nottingham, history timeline
- University of Nottingham, Going Global exhibition
- University of Nottingham Students’ Union, student newsletters
- University of Nottingham, alumni magazine

The Trent Building image is served by the University of Nottingham’s public image CDN. Other photos are from Unsplash and are used as illustrative stock imagery. Check asset permissions and replace any image as required by the University’s brand and licensing guidelines.

## Local preview

Serve the `dist/` directory with any static web server, or open `dist/index.html` directly in a browser.
